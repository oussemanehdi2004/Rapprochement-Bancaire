1. INTRODUCTION: What is this system?

Imagine you work in a bank and you have to check thousands of transactions every day to find the bad ones (fraud). This would be impossible to do manually!

This system is like having a super-smart assistant that:

🔍 AUTOMATICALLY FINDS SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTIONS
(like someone stealing money)

🏦 COMBINES DATA FROM MULTIPLE BANKS
(so you can see everything in one place)

📊 SHOWS YOU CLEAR REPORTS
(so you can understand what's happening)

⚙️ LETS YOU ADJUST THE RULES
(so you can customize what to look for)

3. 🔍 FRAUD DETECTION PAGE - The Security Guard Room

GOAL: This is like having a security guard who watches every transaction and says "This looks suspicious!" or "This looks normal."

The page has 6 different tabs (like 6 different tools in your security kit):

📊 TAB 1: OVERVIEW (Vue d'ensemble) - The Dashboard

GOAL: See everything important at a glance, like a car dashboard.

WHAT YOU SEE:

Big Numbers at the top: - Total Analyzed: How many transactions we checked today - Fraud Rate: What percentage might be bad - Total at Risk: How much money might be stolen
- Global Risk Score: Overall danger level (0-100)

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine you have a store. At the end of the day, you look at: - 100 customers came in - 5 of them looked suspicious - €2,000 worth of goods might have been stolen - Overall risk level: 15% (not too bad)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Look at the big numbers to understand today's situation
  2. If you see red/high numbers, you know you need to investigate
  3. Click on other tabs to see details

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Quick health check of your banking system
  • Helps you decide where to focus your attention
  • Shows if fraud is increasing or decreasing

🔍 TAB 2: HYBRID DETECTION (Détection Hybride) - The Investigation List

GOAL: See each suspicious transaction and understand why it was flagged.

WHAT YOU SEE:

A table of transactions with columns: - Date: When it happened - Description: What the transaction is - Amount: How much money (€15,000) - Score: How suspicious (0-100, higher = more dangerous) - Category: What type of problem - Status: What to do (needs investigation, cleared, etc.)

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine you're checking receipts at a store: - Receipt #1: €45 for office supplies ✅ (normal) - Receipt #2: €15,000 to unknown person ⚠️ (suspicious!) - Receipt #3: €500 for cash withdrawal at 2 AM ⚠️ (suspicious!)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Look at the list of flagged transactions
  2. Click on filters to see only certain types
  3. Search for specific transactions
  4. Click on a transaction to see detailed explanation

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Shows exactly which transactions need human review
  • Helps you prioritize (check dangerous ones first)
  • Provides evidence for why something was flagged

🕸️ TAB 3: NETWORKS & GRAPH (Réseaux & Graphe) - The Connection Map

GOAL: See how accounts are connected to each other (like a family tree but for money).

WHAT YOU SEE:

A visual map with dots and lines: - Dots = Bank accounts - Lines = Money transfers between accounts
- Red = Suspicious transfers - Blue = Normal transfers

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine investigating a crime: - Person A sends money to Person B - Person B sends money to Person C
- Person C sends money back to Person A - This circle looks suspicious! 🔄

HOW TO USE:

  1. Select a suspicious account from the list
  2. See who they send money to (their "friends")
  3. Look for patterns like:
  4. Money going in circles (circular payments)
  5. One account receiving from many people (might be a "mule" account)
  6. Accounts that only appear once (new accounts, might be fake)

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Finds organized fraud networks (not just single bad transactions)
  • Identifies "money mule" accounts (people used to hide stolen money)
  • Shows relationships between different banks and customers

📈 TAB 4: SHAP EXPLAINABILITY (Explicabilité SHAP) - The "Why" Detector

GOAL: Understand EXACTLY why the computer thinks something is fraud.

WHAT YOU SEE:

For each suspicious transaction, the system shows: - Which factors made it suspicious - How important each factor is (like "Amount: 65% important") - Positive vs Negative contributions

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine a doctor explaining why you're sick: - "You have a fever" (major factor) - "You have a cough" (minor factor)
- "You're tired" (contributing factor) The doctor explains exactly what's wrong and why.

HOW TO USE:

  1. Look at the chart showing which factors matter most
  2. See if the reasons make sense (sometimes the computer makes mistakes!)
  3. Use this to decide if you should block or allow the transaction
  4. Learn patterns to improve your fraud detection

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Builds trust in the system (you can see why decisions are made)
  • Helps train staff to recognize fraud patterns
  • Allows you to fine-tune the rules based on real examples

📋 TAB 5: BUSINESS RULES (Règles Métier) - The Rule Book

GOAL: See which types of fraud are most common and their patterns.

WHAT YOU SEE:

A breakdown of fraud categories: - SEUIL_REGLEMENTAIRE: Transactions over €10,000 (legal requirement) - RETRAIT_CASH_IMPORTANT: Large cash withdrawals (over €5,000) - MOTCLE_SENSIBLE: Transactions with suspicious words - FRACTIONNEMENT: Someone splitting big payments into small ones - NOUVEL_IBAN: First time sending money to this person

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine a store security report: - 10 people tried to steal (shoplifting) - 5 people used fake credit cards - 3 people returned stolen items for refunds - 2 people worked together (one distracted, one stole)

HOW TO USE:

  1. See which types of fraud happen most often
  2. Focus your prevention efforts on the biggest problems
  3. Understand if your rules are working
  4. Adjust your security based on patterns

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Helps you understand your fraud landscape
  • Shows if criminals are changing their tactics
  • Guides where to put security resources

⚙️ TAB 6: CONFIG THRESHOLDS (Config Seuils) - The Control Panel

GOAL: Adjust the sensitivity of fraud detection (like setting the sensitivity of a car alarm).

WHAT YOU SEE:

Sliders and input fields to change: - SEUIL_REGLEMENTAIRE: At what amount to flag (€10,000, €15,000, etc.) - SEUIL_APPROCHE_RATIO: How close to the limit before warning (90%, 80%) - SEUIL_CASH_OUT: When to worry about cash withdrawals - MOTS_CLES_SENSIBLES: Which words to watch for (CASINO, PARIS, etc.)

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine setting a house alarm: - How loud should it be? (sensitivity) - Which doors should it monitor? (coverage) - What should trigger it? (motion, sound, door opening?) - When should it call the police? (threshold)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Adjust thresholds based on your business needs
  2. Add new suspicious words as you discover them
  3. Make it stricter if you're missing fraud
  4. Make it more lenient if you're getting too many false alarms

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Customizes the system for your specific bank
  • Balances security vs. customer convenience
  • Adapts to new fraud patterns as they emerge

4. 🏦 MULTI-BANKING PAGE - The Central Hub

GOAL: This is like having a super assistant who can read bank statements from different banks and put them all in one place.

📤 FILE UPLOAD WIDGET - The Document Reader

GOAL: Upload bank files from different banks to analyze them.

WHAT YOU SEE:

  • File selection button (to choose your file)
  • Bank selection dropdown (which bank is this from?)
  • Format selection (what type of file is this?)
  • Upload button (send it for analysis)

SUPPORTED FILE TYPES:

  • CSV: Like Excel files (most common)
  • CAMT053: Special banking format (European standard)
  • MT940: Another banking format (international standard)
  • PAIN001: Payment initiation format

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Imagine you have statements from: - Bank A: PDF file - Bank B: Excel file
- Bank C: Special banking file This system can read ALL of them and combine the information!

HOW TO USE:

  1. Click "Choose File" and select your bank statement
  2. Select which bank it's from (BNP, Société Générale, etc.)
  3. Select the file format (CSV, CAMT053, etc.)
  4. Click "Upload"
  5. Wait for processing (usually a few seconds)
  6. See results showing how many transactions were found

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Centralizes data: No more switching between different bank systems
  • Standardizes formats: Makes all banks look the same
  • Automates processing: No more manual data entry
  • Enables cross-bank analysis: Can spot patterns across different banks

📊 STATISTICS WIDGET - The Scoreboard

GOAL: See how many files you've processed and how successful it was.

WHAT YOU SEE:

  • Total Files: How many bank statements you've uploaded
  • Successful: How many were processed correctly
  • Failed: How many had errors
  • Total Transactions: Total number of transactions across all files

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Like a sports scoreboard: - Games played: 10 (files uploaded) - Games won: 8 (successful) - Games lost: 2 (failed) - Total points: 1,500 (transactions processed)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Check the numbers to see your processing progress
  2. If you see many failures, check your file formats
  3. Monitor transaction counts to ensure data is being captured

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Tracks productivity: How much work you've done
  • Identifies problems: High failure rate means something needs fixing
  • Planning: Helps estimate processing capacity

📋 RECENT UPLOADS TABLE - The History Log

GOAL: See your recent uploads and their status.

WHAT YOU SEE:

  • Filename: What you uploaded
  • Bank: Which bank it was from
  • Format: What type of file
  • Status: Success or failure
  • Transaction Count: How many transactions were in the file
  • Upload Time: When you uploaded it

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Like a package delivery log: - Package 1: Delivered ✅ (successful) - Package 2: Failed ❌ (address wrong) - Package 3: Delivered ✅ (successful)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Review recent uploads to see what worked and what didn't
  2. Click on failed uploads to see error messages
  3. Re-upload failed files after fixing problems
  4. Track your work history

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Audit trail: See what was processed and when
  • Troubleshooting: Identify recurring problems
  • Workflow management: Track team progress

5. 📋 TRANSACTIONS PAGE - The Search Engine

GOAL: This is like Google but for bank transactions - you can search and filter through all your transactions.

🔍 FILTERS - The Search Tools

GOAL: Find specific transactions quickly.

WHAT YOU CAN FILTER BY:

Status: - MATCHED: Transactions that balanced out correctly - UNMATCHED: Transactions that don't have a matching pair - SUSPICIOUS: Transactions that might be fraud

Date Range: From/to specific dates
Search: Text search (description, amount, account numbers)

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Like searching through emails: - Show me only unread emails (status filter) - Show me emails from last week (date filter)
- Show me emails with "invoice" in the subject (search filter)

HOW TO USE:

  1. Choose what you want to filter (status, dates, or search)
  2. Enter your criteria
  3. Wait a moment for results to appear
  4. Browse the filtered list

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Quick research: Find specific transactions instantly
  • Investigation: Focus on problem areas
  • Reconciliation: Find unmatched transactions that need attention

📊 TRANSACTIONS TABLE - The Master List

GOAL: See all your transactions in one organized list.

WHAT YOU SEE:

  • Transaction details: ID, date, description, amount
  • Status indicators: Color-coded (green for matched, red for suspicious)
  • Account information: Which accounts were involved
  • Reconciliation status: Whether the books balance

SIMPLE EXAMPLE:

Like a checkbook register:

Date Description Amount Status
01/01 Salary deposit +€2,500 ✅ Matched
01/02 Rent payment -€900 ✅ Matched
01/03 Unknown transfer -€5,000 ⚠️ Suspicious

HOW TO USE:

  1. Browse through all transactions
  2. Use filters to focus on specific ones
  3. Click on transactions for more details
  4. Export data if needed for reports

WHY IT'S USEFUL FOR BANKMATCH:

  • Complete visibility: See all financial activity
  • Reconciliation: Ensure accounts balance
  • Audit: Complete record of all transactions

7. WHY THESE MODULES ARE CRUCIAL FOR BANKMATCH/RAPPROCHEMENT BANCAIRE

THE PROBLEM: What Banks Face

Banks have to handle millions of transactions every day while: - ⚠️ Preventing fraud (criminals trying to steal money) - 🏦 Following regulations (laws about reporting suspicious activity) - 🔗 Matching transactions (ensuring money goes to the right places) - 📊 Providing reports (showing regulators everything is legal)

HOW FRAUD DETECTION HELPS

1. AUTOMATIC PROTECTION

  • Before: Humans had to check every transaction manually (impossible!)
  • After: AI checks every transaction instantly (100% coverage)

Real Impact: - A bank processes 1 million transactions per day - Humans could maybe check 1,000 (0.1% coverage) - AI checks all 1,000,000 (100% coverage) - Result: 999,000 more transactions get protected!

2. NETWORK DETECTION

  • Before: Could only spot individual bad transactions
  • After: Can spot entire criminal networks working together

Real Impact: - Criminals use 50 fake accounts to hide money - Old system: Might catch 1-2 accounts - New system: Catches the entire network of 50 accounts - Result: Much more money saved!

3. EXPLAINABLE DECISIONS

  • Before: Computer says "fraud" but doesn't explain why
  • After: Computer explains exactly why (amount too high, unusual time, etc.)

Real Impact: - Staff can make better decisions - Fewer false alarms (less inconvenience to honest customers) - Better training for new staff

4. ADJUSTABLE RULES

  • Before: Fixed rules that couldn't adapt
  • After: Flexible rules that can be customized per bank

Real Impact: - Each bank has different customers and risk profiles - Can tune the system to your specific needs - Adapts quickly to new fraud techniques

HOW MULTI-BANKING HELPS

1. CENTRALIZED DATA

  • Before: Had to log into 10 different bank systems
  • After: All bank data in one place

Real Impact: - Time saved: Instead of 10 logins, just 1 - Better analysis: Can see patterns across banks - Fewer errors: No manual data transfer between systems

2. STANDARDIZED FORMATS

  • Before: Each bank uses different file formats
  • After: System reads all formats automatically

Real Impact: - No more manual data entry - Fewer mistakes from copying data - Faster processing (minutes instead of days)

3. CROSS-BANK ANALYSIS

  • Before: Could only see one bank at a time
  • After: Can analyze across all banks simultaneously

Real Impact: - Criminals often use multiple banks - Can spot money moving between banks - Better compliance with anti-money-laundering laws

4. WORKFLOW MANAGEMENT

  • Before: No tracking of what was processed
  • After: Complete history and status tracking

Real Impact: - Audit trail for regulators - Team coordination (who did what) - Problem identification (recurring issues)

8. A DAY IN THE LIFE - Practical Example

SCENARIO: A Bank Accountant's Morning

8:00 AM - Arrive at work, open the system

  • Check Fraud Detection Overview
  • See 3 suspicious transactions overnight
  • Priority: A €50,000 transfer to a new account looks dangerous

8:15 AM - Investigate the suspicious transfer

  • Go to Hybrid Detection tab
  • See the transfer was flagged because:
  • Amount over €10,000 limit
  • First time sending to this beneficiary
  • Unusual time (3 AM)

8:30 AM - Check connections

  • Go to Network Graph tab
  • See the beneficiary account has received money from 20 other accounts
  • This looks like a money laundering network!

8:45 AM - Take action

  • Flag the transaction for investigation
  • Block the account temporarily
  • Notify compliance team

9:00 AM - Process daily bank statements

  • Go to Multi-Banking page
  • Upload statements from 5 different banks
  • System processes 15,000 transactions automatically
  • All transactions matched and balanced ✅

9:30 AM - Check weekly report

  • Go to Reports page
  • Generate weekly fraud report
  • See fraud rate decreased from 2% to 0.5% (system working!)
  • Export PDF for management meeting

10:00 AM - Morning coffee ☕

  • System caught €200,000 in potential fraud
  • Saved hours of manual work
  • Bank is safer and more efficient
